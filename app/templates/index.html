Why open ExpenseAI

The first screen should tell you what the app does. These are the three reasons to make an account.

✎

Categories in your colors

Record an expense, pick a category, and give that category a color you like. Food can be pink, travel yellow, study brown. Lists and charts stay sorted by those colors, so a glance is enough.

💬

AI financial chat assistant

Ask in plain words: what did I spend, which category grew, what can I set aside. The assistant answers from your own expenses, not from a blank page.

◈

AI analysis

Open Analysis for a calm summary: top categories, a simple trend, and one next step. It is built to be read in a minute, not studied like a spreadsheet.

A reason to use it

  • Logging a cost takes one form: amount, category, and the color already attached to that category.
  • Colors are yours. Change them any time and the history keeps the new look.
  • The chat assistant is there when you want an answer, not another chart to decode.
  • AI Analysis turns the week into a short note you can act on.
